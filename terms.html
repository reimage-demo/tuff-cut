About this website

This website provides information about Tuff Cut Barbershop at 1103 Albany Avenue, Hartford, CT 06112. You may browse our services, find contact details, and view in-store payment information.

Services, prices, and availability

Prices on this website are transcribed from the shop’s posted service board. Services, prices, barber availability, and opening hours may change. Confirm the service, total price, and availability with your barber before your visit or service.

Calling the shop does not itself confirm an appointment. Please agree on any appointment directly with your barber.

In-store payments

Confirm the recipient and amount with your barber before paying. Any enabled payment links open a third-party payment provider. That provider’s terms apply to its service. This website does not process payments, store card details, or independently confirm payment completion.

For questions about a charge, receipt, or service, contact the shop. This website does not establish a separate cancellation or refund policy.

Using the site

Please use the website lawfully and do not attempt to interfere with its operation. If a page is unavailable or information seems incorrect, call the shop for assistance.
