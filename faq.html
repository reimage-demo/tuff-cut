Barbershop FAQ

Where is Tuff Cut Barbershop in Hartford?

You’ll find us at 1103 Albany Avenue, Hartford, CT 06112. Look for the Tuff Cuts Barbershop sign above the front window. See the storefront.

Can I walk in for a haircut on Albany Avenue?

Yes. Tuff Cut welcomes walk-ins seven days a week. For a particular barber or to ask about availability before you come in, call (860) 794-6550. Call the shop.

What time does the barbershop open on Sundays?

Sunday hours are 11 AM to 8 PM. Monday through Saturday, the shop is open from 9 AM to 8 PM. Holiday hours may vary. See hours and directions.

How much is a regular haircut at Tuff Cut?

The posted prices for a regular cut are $20 for kids, $25 for men and $30 for women. A men’s cut and shave is $30. Confirm your service and total with your barber. View the full service board and prices.

Can I get a fade, taper or lineup?

Yes. The haircut gallery shows skin fades, tapers, waves, lineups and beard shaping. Bring a reference photo if you have a particular finish in mind. A standalone lineup is $15 on the shop’s service board. See haircuts from the shop.

Can I get a beard shave or a lineup without a full haircut?

Yes. The shop lists a beard shave for $15, a lineup for $15, and a lineup and shave together for $20. See services and prices.

Does Tuff Cut offer kids’ haircuts?

Yes. A regular kids’ haircut is $20. Families can walk in during shop hours or call before visiting. Meet the barbers.

How do I pay for my haircut in the shop?

Confirm the total with your barber, then use the Cash App or Zelle link or QR code on the in-store payment page. Show your barber the confirmation after you pay. Open in-store payment.
