Accessibility

Updated October 19, 2026

A website you can use

We want everyone to be able to find shop information, check prices, and contact a barber. This site is designed with readable text, clear contrast, keyboard navigation, descriptive image alternatives, and layouts that adapt to smaller screens.

Finding your way

  • Use the “Skip to content” link at the beginning of each page to reach the main content.
  • Navigate links and buttons with the Tab key. A visible outline indicates keyboard focus.
  • On smaller screens, open the navigation with the three-line menu button. Escape closes it.
  • Use your browser’s zoom controls to make text larger. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Payment assistance

QR codes may require a separate device or app. When an owner-provided payment link is available, it will also appear as a text link. You can always ask your barber for assistance at the counter.

Feedback and support

If you have trouble using this website, call (860) 794-6550 or speak with us at 1103 Albany Avenue, Hartford, CT 06112. Tell us which page or action caused the problem so we can help.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. This statement describes the features included in the site; it is not a certification of conformance. External maps and payment services may work differently.
