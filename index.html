Meet your barbers.

OWNER & BARBER

Odean

Same neighborhood.
A new chapter.

Odean has spent 20 years at the shop. Before moving here, he owned a barbershop across the street for many years. The address changed. His connection to the neighborhood stayed.

The photographs he holds tell part of that story: family, the old shop, and the people and places that brought him here.

35 YEARS OF EXPERIENCE

Robert Noble

Robert followed his father into barbering, practicing long before turning professional at 19. With 35 years in the business, he brings a versatile skill set and a patient approach to every cut. His philosophy is simple: take your time, pay attention to the details, and never rush the work.

20 YEARS OF EXPERIENCE

Alex

Alex and his brothers started cutting hair because they couldn’t afford haircuts. That drive for self-sufficiency grew into 20 years of barbering experience. He specializes in crisp lineups and sharp finishes, guided by one principle: stay consistent.

BARBER

Sue

Sue is part of the Tuff Cut team. Bring a reference photo or talk with her about the look you have in mind.

31 YEARS OF EXPERIENCE

Purple

Purple found barbering unexpectedly and fell in love with the craft. With 31 years of experience and a year at Tuff Cut, he brings versatility and a love for the freedom the work offers. His approach: stay true to what you do and take pride in every cut.

Sue is represented by an illustration, not a photograph.
